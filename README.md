# About Me
I work at the intersection of diagnostics, microtechnology, and data-driven innovation, with a focus on building integrated analytical systems.

My background combines bioanalytics and diagnostics, microengineering, and computational approaches (data, software, system design). This allows me to work across multiple layer of a project (from bioassays and microfluidics to optics, electronics, automation, and data processing) and to help connect these elements into operational systems. With a background as clinical investigator and sponsor and biosafety officer I could support the conceptualisation of complex human-centric-based projects.

I’m used to working in multidisciplinary environments, where scientific, engineering, and software perspectives need to align to move forward (well I hope so :D).

Alongside the technical aspects, I often take on coordination and leadership roles, particularly in complex contexts. I value clarity, constructive collaboration, and steady execution, especially when projects involve multiple stakeholders and high constraints.

I collaborate with industry, academic, and clinical partners to help translate advanced diagnostic concepts into practical technologies.

Happy to connect around diagnostics, engineering, and scientific topics.
